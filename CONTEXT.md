## What it is

The desk he sits at. It carries the work breakdown, tells him where he is when he
sits down, drives local and cloud processes, and seeds a conversation with any
assistant from his own rows. Everything else it will carry is named and marked.

## Layout

| path | |
|---|---|
| `mez` | launcher |
| `bin/mez.py` | the whole desk, standard library only |
| `$MEZ_HOME` (`~/.mez`) | `WBS.csv` · `state.json` · `owned.json` |

`owned.json` records which cells he set himself. That is the only reason an
import can be safe by default.

## Bearings

Not a wellness feature and not medical advice. It reports elapsed time at the
desk, time since a logged break, what is unblocked, what is cheapest
(built-but-never-run), what only he can do, and what is blocked and by what — so
the first ten minutes of a session are not spent rebuilding the picture.
